What to label

Up to eight values, each with a button above the tile. Fields left empty take the project's saved settings when it loads again.

Model:

Tap an object, or drag around it. The bright cells inside the yellow line are the cells volunteers marked.

Output

With values, pick one above the tile, or press its number key, before you tap each object. Tap a label to give it the picked value.
